Before You

The story of who I was before you came into my life.

Chapter I

Who I Am

My name is Alex.

I am a kind, calm and caring person. I always try to care about the people who are important to me and make them feel good.

I love walking around the city, watching anime, and making other people happy.

One of the things I want most in life is to help people. I want to be someone who can make another person's life a little easier.

I have dreams. I want to keep moving forward and become the person I want to be.

Chapter II

Where I Came From

I was born in Zhytomyr, Ukraine, on June 21, 2006.

When I was a child, I was quiet. I mostly kept things inside and watched what was happening around me.

I remember trips to Lake Svitiaz with my father. I remember going to the cinema and parks with my brothers.

Then I lost my father. Losing him changed me.

I still remember his words:

Do good for the person you value, and always protect them.

Life was not always kind to me. But I did not break. I kept going.

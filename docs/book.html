My Story

Before You

This book is not only about the things that happened between us.

It is about me.

About where I came from, what I experienced, what hurt me, what I achieved, and what I still dream about.

Maybe there are parts of me you don't know yet.

So I wanted you to see them.

Chapter I

Who I Am

My name is Alex.

I am a kind, calm and caring person. I always try to care about the people who are important to me and make them feel good.

Sometimes I can be stubborn about some things, but I know that this is also a part of who I am.

I love walking around the city, watching anime, and making other people happy.

Sometimes, seeing someone smile because of something I did means more to me than anything else.

Chapter I

Who I Am

One of the things I want most in life is to help people.

I want to be someone who can make another person's life a little easier, even if it is just through small things.

I also want to live beside the person I truly love.

I don't need a perfect life. I just want to have someone important beside me, share ordinary days together, help each other and keep moving forward.

Right now, I may be a little tired of life. There are moments when everything feels difficult. But I know that I cannot stop here.

I have dreams.

I want to change my laziness. I want to stop wasting the time I have and dedicate myself to the things I truly want.

Chapter II

Where I Came From

I was born in Zhytomyr, Ukraine, on June 21, 2006.

My zodiac sign is Taurus.

When I was a child, I was quiet. I was not the loudest person in the room. I mostly kept things inside and watched what was happening.

Some of my childhood memories are connected with my family.

I remember trips to Lake Svitiaz with my father. I remember going to the cinema and parks with my brothers.

These may seem like simple moments, but they became part of my memories and part of who I am.

4
Chapter II

Where I Came From

Then I lost my father.

Losing him changed me.

Do good for the person you value, and always protect them.

I still remember those words.

My independent life started when I was sixteen, when I got my first job.

From that moment, I started learning what it means to take responsibility for myself and my own life.

Life was not always kind to me.

I experienced betrayal from friends. I experienced loneliness. I lost people who were important to me.

5
Chapter II

Still Here

There were periods when life became very difficult for me emotionally.

But I did not break.

I kept going.

Even without what I would call a normal education, I still managed to build my own path and show people that I could become someone they could look up to.

Maybe my story is not perfect.

Maybe I made many mistakes.

But everything I went through made me stronger.

I am still here.

Still moving forward.

Still trying to become better than the person I was yesterday.

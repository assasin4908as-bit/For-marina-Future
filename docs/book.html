Chapter I

Who I Am

My name is Oksy.

I am a kind, calm and caring person. I always try to care about the people who are important to me and make them feel good.

Sometimes I can be stubborn about some things, but I know that this is also a part of who I am.

I love walking around the city, watching anime, and making other people happy.

Sometimes, seeing someone smile because of something I did means more to me than anything else.

One of the things I want most in life is to help people. I want to be someone who can make another person's life a little easier.

I don't need a perfect life. I just want to build a life that feels real, meaningful and worth living.

I have dreams.

I want to change my laziness. I want to stop wasting the time I have and dedicate myself to the things I truly want.

I don't know exactly where my life will take me. But I know that I want to keep moving forward.

Chapter II

Where I Came From

I was born in Zhytomyr, Ukraine, in April 2006.

When I was a child, I was quiet. I was not the loudest person in the room. I mostly kept things inside and watched what was happening around me.

Some childhood memories

I remember trips to Lake Svitiaz with my father. I remember going to the cinema and parks with my brothers.

These may seem like simple moments, but they became part of my memories and part of who I am.

Then I lost my father.

Losing him changed me.

"Do good for the person you value, and always protect them."

I still remember those words.

My independent life started when I was sixteen, when I got my first job.

From that moment, I started learning what it means to take responsibility for myself and my own life.

Life was not always kind to me.

I experienced betrayal from friends. I experienced loneliness. I lost people who were important to me.

But I did not break.

I kept going.

The Things That Hurt

Some things are easier to hide in the dark.

If you want to know me, you have to walk through the places I don't usually show.

The Boy

I am afraid of losing someone close to me.

There are people who become so important to us that the thought of losing them becomes one of our biggest fears.

⚔️

The Wounded Knight

Betrayal always hurts the most when it comes from someone you trusted.

I learned that trust can be broken in a moment.

Sometimes the deepest wounds are caused by people we believed would never hurt us.

The Grave

The people close to me are gone, but I will always remember them.

Some people leave our lives, but they never leave our memories.

I carry their memories with me.

The Light

Never give up.

There were moments when life felt too heavy.

There were moments when I could have stopped walking.

But I didn't.

I kept going.

The Other Side

The boy I saw in the darkness is waiting for me again.

But this time, he isn't alone.

He walks forward and shows me the way.

Step by step, until we reach the other side.

Still Walking

Life doesn't end here.

The forest is behind me now. The darkness is behind me.

I don't know what waits ahead. I don't know what the future will bring.

But I know one thing:

I will keep walking.

There is always another road ahead. And as long as I can take another step, my story isn't over.

The Life I Built

After everything I went through, my life started to change.

I began building my own path.

It wasn't always easy, but every step taught me something new.

Maybe some of my achievements don't look huge from the outside.

But I know how much work it took to get there.

I started with very little. I made mistakes. I learned. I kept working.

And I became someone who could take responsibility for himself.

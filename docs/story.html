The beginning

Before there was an "us"

Sometimes the most important people enter our lives in the most ordinary ways. For us, it started with a simple meeting on Badoo. Neither of us knew where that first conversation would eventually lead. But something made us keep talking.

27 June — The First Date

Our first date. We went shopping together. It was not some huge cinematic moment. It was simply time together. Walking around, talking, getting to know each other, and slowly becoming comfortable with each other's presence.

Between the Dates

Then came the waiting. Waiting for another meeting. Missing each other. Thinking about when we would see each other again. And somehow even the waiting became part of our story. Because knowing that another day together was coming made the distance feel different.

18 July — The Second Date

Our second date. LEGO. Another day together. And something else that became important to us — creating a shared bank account. It may sound like a small thing. But for me, it felt like another little step toward building something together.

The Gifts

Over time, I started preparing little surprises for you. Some were bought. Some were made by my own hands. The important thing was never the price. It was the thought behind them. I wanted to give you something that would make you smile.

31 August — The Third Date

Cinema. Another day together. And then I finally said what had been growing inside me. I love you. That day, our relationship changed. We stopped being just two people who were getting to know each other. We became a couple.

A Promise to Keep Learning

There is also something I started because of you. English. I know the language barrier can make things difficult sometimes. So I decided to keep learning. Not because I need to become perfect. But because I want to understand you better. I want to be able to talk with you without words getting in the way. And I promised myself that I would keep going.

We have already created memories. But there are still many pages that haven't been written. More walks. More conversations. More ordinary days. More surprises. More memories. I don't know exactly what every next page will look like. But I'm happy that the story is still continuing.

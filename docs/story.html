Prologue

Before the first date

We met on Badoo.

At first, we were just two people talking.

Neither of us knew where it would lead.

But somehow, our conversations became important.

27 June

Our First Date

Our first date was simple.

We went shopping together.

Nothing needed to be perfect.

We were simply together.

And that was enough to make the day special.

Between the dates

The Waiting

Sometimes the hardest part is waiting.

Waiting for the next meeting. Missing someone. Thinking about them when they are far away.

But there was something beautiful about knowing that another meeting was waiting for us.

18 July

LEGO & Something More

Our second date was different.

We built LEGO together.

We also opened a shared bank account.

It may sound like a small thing, but for me it became another memory that I wanted to keep.

Little things

Gifts

Some gifts are not really about the things themselves.

They are about the thought behind them.

Every little thing I gave you had a meaning.

I wanted you to have something that could remind you of me.

31 August

The Day Everything Changed

We went to the cinema.

We spent time together.

And then I finally said what I had been feeling.

I love you.

That day became more than another date.

We started our relationship.

A promise

Learning English for you 🇬🇧❤️

There is something else I started doing because of you.

I started learning English.

I know that language can sometimes make things difficult.

So I want to become better.

I want to understand you better. Talk to you more easily. And be able to share more with you.

I promised myself that I will keep learning.

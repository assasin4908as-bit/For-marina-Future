How It Started

Sometimes a story begins without either person knowing how important it will become.

Oksy and Marina met through Badoo. At first, it was simply two people talking, getting to know each other and discovering little things about one another.

Neither of us could know what the next few months would bring.

But somewhere between the messages, the waiting, the meetings, and all the small moments, something started to grow.

And this is where our story begins. ❤️
